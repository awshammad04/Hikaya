## 5. Important facts
- The Great Omari Mosque stands on a site venerated for centuries, first a pagan temple and then a Byzantine church; after the Islamic conquest in 634 it became the focal point of the Islamic city. The Mamluks and Ottomans enlarged the mosque and rebuilt it around a Crusader basilica incorporated into its structure. The nomination describes a tall minaret, an open courtyard, a marble mihrab and a Gothic-style entrance arch, calls it Gaza's main congregational mosque beside the Qaysariyya and Zawiya souqs, and notes damage from repeated wars across the historic centre. The historic-centre nomination was submitted in 2026; it is a Tentative List entry, not World Heritage inscription.
