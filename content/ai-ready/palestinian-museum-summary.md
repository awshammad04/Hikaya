## 1. Short overview
A cultural institution in Birzeit beside Birzeit University, documenting Palestinian history and culture.

## 5. Important facts
- The idea began in 1997, when members of the Taawon-Welfare Association set out to create a museum dedicated to the memory of the Nakba; it later grew into an institution celebrating Palestinian culture more broadly. It was built in Birzeit rather than Jerusalem because of the occupation's restrictions on Palestinian building in the city and on movement to it, on a 40,000 m² plot offered by Birzeit University seven kilometres north of Ramallah. The Dublin firm Heneghan Peng won the 2011 international design competition with a building set into terraces that follow the hillside, and the first phase, 3,500 m², was completed in 2016. The building holds a LEED Gold certificate, and the museum launched without a collection, relying on touring exhibitions and digital platforms.
